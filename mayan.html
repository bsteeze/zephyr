Why two answers?

The traditional Tzolk'in has run unbroken for more than two thousand years and is still kept in Guatemala. Dreamspell is a 20th-century reinterpretation with its own colors, tones and "oracle" relationships. It's popular, but it doesn't line up with the ancient count.

Neither one is "wrong." Each is a different lens. Zephyr shows both and lets you notice which one rings true.
