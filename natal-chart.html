Questions people ask

Natal chart basics.

What information is needed for a natal chart?

Your birth date, exact local birth time, and birth city or coordinates. Time and location are essential for calculating the Rising sign, Midheaven, and houses.

Is the Zephyr natal chart free?

Yes. The chart generator is free to use in a supported web browser.

Does Zephyr store my birth information?

Saved profiles are stored locally in your browser. Zephyr does not send birth details to a third-party astrology calculation API.

What makes Zephyr different?

Zephyr is a first-of-its-kind interactive observatory bringing together a visual natal chart, approachable interpretation, and a solar harmony instrument that turns birthday spacing into musical intervals.
