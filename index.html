Frequently Asked Questions

What is a Solar Harmonic Interval?

It is Zephyr’s name for the musical distance created when two birthday positions in the solar year are mapped onto one octave.

How are birthdays converted into music?

Zephyr measures the spacing between dates within the solar year and maps that proportion onto 1,200 cents—the distance of one musical octave.

Does Zephyr use astrology?

The zodiac appears as an optional calendar layer. The musical calculations themselves are based on date positions and music theory.

Can I compare more than two people?

Yes. Add multiple birthdays to hear them together as a chord and explore the interval relationships within the group.
